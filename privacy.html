Your privacy matters to us. We collect only what we need, never sell your data, and give you full control over your information.

1. What we collect

When you use Swiftfolio, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name and email address when you create an account
  • CV content you enter into the builder — name, job title, experience, education, skills
  • Profile photo if you choose to upload one
  • Payment information processed securely via Paystack — we never store card details
  • Basic usage data such as pages visited and features used

2. How we use your data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the Swiftfolio service:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To save and retrieve your CVs
  • To process your payments
  • To send important account notifications — no spam
  • To improve the product based on how it is used

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not build marketing profiles.

3. How we store your data

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which provides industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit. CV drafts are also saved locally in your browser using localStorage for convenience.

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed within 30 days.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We work with the following trusted third parties only as necessary to operate the service:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Paystack — payment processing
  • Vercel — hosting and delivery

Each of these providers operates under their own privacy policies and is bound by data protection agreements.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your CV data at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

6. Cookies

Swiftfolio uses minimal cookies — only those necessary for the service to function, such as keeping you signed in. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Your theme preference (light or dark mode) is stored in your browser's localStorage, not a cookie.

7. Children

Swiftfolio is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Swiftfolio after changes are made constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

privacy@swiftfolio.com
